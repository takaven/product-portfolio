# Product Definition

Canonical state lives in `../../PORTFOLIO.yaml`.

## Promise

A people-operations product for structured HR workflows and employee lifecycle management.

## Buyer

HR lead or operator responsible for employee operations.

## Problem

People operations require reliable workflows, records and management visibility after employment begins.

## Product Boundary

- employee lifecycle workflows
- people operations records
- post-hire HR operations

## Explicit Exclusions

- pre-hire recruitment pass workflows owned by HirePass
- property operations
- payroll calculation engine

## Relationship To TeamFrame

self

## Remarks

Portfolio record exists for visibility and boundary control. Do not create TeamFrame execution issues from this setup.
