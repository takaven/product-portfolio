# Product Definition

Canonical state lives in `../../PORTFOLIO.yaml`.

## Promise

A payroll change-control and document-intelligence layer for intake, extraction, approvals, variance checks and auditability.

## Buyer

HR/payroll operator responsible for controlled payroll changes.

## Problem

Payroll change inputs arrive through documents and manual messages, creating variance, approval and audit risk.

## Product Boundary

- incoming payroll change documents
- document extraction
- classification
- employee change records
- approval workflow
- payroll variance control
- auditability
- exports

## Explicit Exclusions

- full payroll system
- payroll calculation engine
- replacement for mature payroll suites
- tax filing engine

## Relationship To TeamFrame

conditional; could be TeamFrame add-on or independent control-layer product

## Remarks

Must not become full payroll software. Boundary is payroll change control and document intelligence.
