# Product Definition

Canonical state lives in `../../PORTFOLIO.yaml`.

## Promise

A simple operating system for small commercial landlords to manage units, tenants, leases, rent, arrears, documents and expenses.

## Buyer

Small commercial landlord or family-owned commercial property operator.

## Problem

Small commercial portfolios are often run through spreadsheets, email and scattered documents.

## Product Boundary

- commercial units and stores
- tenant and lease records
- rent and payment status
- arrears
- documents
- expenses
- basic reporting and settings

## Explicit Exclusions

- enterprise property management
- online rent collection
- maintenance ticketing
- advanced accounting
- tenant marketplace

unrelated
